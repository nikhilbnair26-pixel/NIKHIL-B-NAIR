Why I Created NIKHIL Studio

"I built this auto-cutter to save hours editing my Minecraft gameplay videos."

Hey guys! I'm Nikhil B Nair, running the Malayalam gaming channel @nikhilminecraftmalayalam. During long Minecraft survival & build sessions, I noticed that manually trimming silent mining, walking, and repeated voice takes was taking up hours of my time.

Most auto-cutting programs either require a high-end graphics card (GPU), force you to pay expensive monthly subscriptions, or require uploading private footage to cloud servers. I coded **NIKHIL Video Studio** in Python so creators on budget and medium PCs can automatically clean up footage offline—fast, free, and local.

Tool Capabilities

Everything You Need To Fast-Track Edits

Designed specifically for gaming creators running OBS recordings on medium & budget PCs.

Smart Dead-Air Cutting

Removes silent pauses, long boring walks, and non-active gameplay stretches with precision audio threshold matching.

Low-CPU Noise Clean

Suppresses background PC fan hiss and mic room hum locally on CPU without dragging down your machine or using GPU.

Retake & Stutter Trim

Detects repeated false starts or stuttered words and keeps only your last clean take automatically.

Smart BGM Auto-Ducking

Automatically lowers background music when commentary starts and raises it during gameplay action.
