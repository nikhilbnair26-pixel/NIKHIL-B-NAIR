System Requirements

Minimum Specs

4-core CPU (Intel Core i3 class or equivalent), 8 GB RAM, integrated or entry-level graphics, 64-bit OS, and stable internet.

Recommended for Smooth Play & Recording

Multi-core CPU, 8 GB–16 GB RAM, dedicated GPU or tuned graphics settings, performance mods, and enough free storage for recordings.

Editing & Workflow Notes

Close heavy background apps while recording or rendering, keep graphics drivers updated, and test performance settings per game or modpack.
